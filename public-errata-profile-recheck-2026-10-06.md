# Public PDF/A-4 and PDF/X-6 errata recheck — 2026-10-06

**Purpose:** inspect current public errata and profile-index resources for version status, clause coverage, and revision boundaries. These resources are corroboration and change-awareness evidence, not substitutes for the controlling standards.

## PDF/A-4

The PDF Association errata page identifies the current target as ISO 19005-4:2020 and organizes resolved corrections under Clause 6, Technical requirements, including Table 2, the PDF/A identification schema:

<https://pdf-issues.pdfa.org/19005-4-2020/>

The page states that a formal dated revision is being prepared and will eventually replace the 2020 edition. The PDF Association's technical resource independently confirms PDF/A-4's PDF 2.0 basis, preservation scope, and the distinction between the current edition and the revision in progress:

<https://pdfa.org/resource/iso-19005-4-pdf-a-4/>

## PDF/X-6

The PDF Association errata page identifies ISO 15930-9:2020, names PDF/X-6, PDF/X-6p, and PDF/X-6n, and states that a formal dated revision is being prepared. It is explicitly a placeholder for industry-approved corrections, with issues tracked in the public `pdf-issues` repository:

<https://pdf-issues.pdfa.org/15930-9-2020/>

The technical index independently lists PDF/X-6, PDF/X-6p, and PDF/X-6n as ISO 15930-9:2020 profiles based on PDF 2.0:

<https://pdfa.org/resource/iso-15930-pdfx/>

## Boundary

The PDF Association's technical-resources page explains that errata are presented as localized marked-up changes with minimal surrounding ISO text because of copyright restrictions:

<https://pdfa.org/resources>

Therefore these pages can update the study's revision and errata awareness, identify clause locations, and support independent source corroboration. They cannot provide a complete requirement map, replace the authorized PDF/A-4 or PDF/X-6 text, or establish a preflight result.

## Disposition

The current published targets remain ISO 19005-4:2020 and ISO 15930-9:2020. Draft dated revisions are tracked separately. The source gate remains `CHANGES_REQUIRED` until the authorized normative texts, complete clause mappings, a PDF/X-6-capable preflight, and required independent checks are present.
