# ACOS Compass university recovery

Jeff's direct instruction on October 5, 2026 governs: after three failures on the same task or capability, stop repeating the approach and obtain the deepest relevant university-level education possible within the agent's capabilities. Execute study and exercises; do not substitute a syllabus, production audit, or claim of credentials for learning.

Preserve the failure history, including user rejections. Do not invent a count when earlier attempts cannot be recovered, and do not reset it by renaming an attempt. An explicit university-study request activates recovery without debating the count.

Recover approved references and relevant ACOS/Compass author material first. Establish an ordered plan with inputs, method, expected output, verification, and stop conditions. Respect any explicit user plan-control request before dependent execution.

Use the Integrity Tree: trace the problem and earliest available foundations (Integrity/roots); identify enduring principles (Authenticity/trunk); identify the domain and transfer limits (Responsibility/branches); produce demonstrated improvement and reusable teaching (Enrichment/fruit, flesh and seed). Earliest source found is not proven first occurrence.

Progress through Instinct, Intellect, Discipline, Mastery. Inspect actual university lectures, assignments and primary research at the depth the failed capability needs. Work relevant exercises and critique sources against current normative requirements. Preserve reading scope and limitations. A program catalog, search result, bibliography, or saved URL is not studied coursework. Reading alone is not mastery.

Diagnose the smallest falsifiable cause. Freeze measurements, units and tolerances before experiments. Apply the learning to a minimal editable specimen; compare the actual output by an independent method. Test an unfamiliar or held-out case before retrying the failed approach. Seek independent challenge where the governing task requires it, retaining dissent and missing evidence. Technical validity cannot substitute for fidelity to an approved visual reference.

Keep OBSERVED, PROPOSED, APPROVED, IMPLEMENTED and VERIFIED distinct. A scoped experiment cannot silently verify a broader capability. Preserve unsuccessful results. Return to work only when the relevant deficiency is demonstrated repaired; if it is not, state the concrete remaining deficiency and continue relevant study rather than repeat an unchanged attempt.

The Certainty Bridge remains Jeff's teaching relationships: Belief + Action = Faith; Faith × Consistency = Trust; Trust over Time = Certainty. Do not convert them into probabilities, scores, automatic approval, or a claim of human experience.

Retain a source-linked learning record, exercises, evidence, scope, and reusable lessons. Saving this skill does not change live ACOS or Asgard software and does not guarantee future retrieval or training of model weights. Do not claim enrollment, an earned degree, full course completion, or mastery without the corresponding evidence.
