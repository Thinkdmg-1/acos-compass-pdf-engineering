## Directly inspected statements

The extracted public text records that:

- ISO 14289-1 defines Tagged PDF requirements for PDF 1.7 and was published in the early development of PDF 2.0 (page 1, lines 15–20).
- The PDF Association's Tagged PDF work includes the Matterhorn Protocol, Tagged PDF Best Practice Guide, PDF/UA Reference Suite, hierarchical inclusion rules, and an accessibility glossary (page 2, lines 27–39).
- “Using Tagged PDF in PDF 2.0” was developed in alignment with ISO TC 171 SC 2 WG 9 to ensure compatibility with the forthcoming ISO 14289-2 (page 2, lines 44–54).
- The document states that the PDF/UA-2 text was developed by PDF Association working groups in coordination with ISO TC 171 SC 2 WG 9 and says committee comments were fed back into that process (page 3, lines 55–59).
- The feature areas named for PDF/UA-2 include comprehensive provisions for annotations and structure-element attributes, PDF 2.0 namespaces integrating PDF 1.7 and PDF 2.0 structure elements, MathML, and the Artifact structure element type (page 3, lines 60–65).

These statements directly corroborate the curriculum's separation of PDF/UA-1 and PDF/UA-2, the need to test namespaces and artifacts, and the use of Matterhorn-style machine/human boundary evidence. They do not expose the complete ISO clauses, conformance conditions, exceptions, or profile text.
