## Admission rule

Only `OBSERVED` or `IMPLEMENTED STUDY` rows can support a current claim, and only within the stated scope. `PROPOSED`, `MISSING`, and `PROPOSED STUDY TOPIC` rows cannot support a conformance or mastery statement. The current matrix therefore keeps the overall source gate `CHANGES_REQUIRED`.

## 2026-10-06 — Type0 descendant font-probe resolution

The PDF Oxide 0.3.78 PDF/X-6 checker was rebuilt with a minimal diagnostic patch that resolves `/Type0` fonts through `/DescendantFonts` before checking the descendant `/FontDescriptor`. On the unchanged specimen, the patched probe reports `embedded_fonts=2` and removes both prior font errors; only the missing `GTS_PDFXVersion` error remains, with Trapped and annotation warnings. This establishes an implementation-resolution correction for the prior font conflict, not ISO conformance or production preflight.
